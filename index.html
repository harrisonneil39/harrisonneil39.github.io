NFL · 2024 season · Machine learning application

Smart Picks

A sports betting prediction tool for NFL player props.

Smart Picks projects a player's output for 12 prop types (passing yards, receptions and more) against a specific opponent and venue. An XGBoost model trained on rolling 3-, 5- and 10-game averages and opponent defensive data produces the projection. When a sportsbook line is entered, 2,000 bootstrap simulations turn it into an over/under pick with a confidence level. Four research views show the recent form, matchup history, home/away split and defensive context behind each prediction.

Supply chain operations · Interactive simulation

SPF Flock Outbreak Scenario Model

If a disease outbreak spreads through an egg production network, what does waiting cost?

An operations dashboard for a specific-pathogen-free (SPF) egg producer with 45 houses across 11 locations, inspired by my internship work in an SPF egg production supply chain. A week-by-week model simulates disease spreading within and between locations and compares four responses (no action, a phased remediation plan, an immediate response and a custom scenario) on lost production and cumulative cost over 52 weeks. A financial view weighs the 12-month cost of inaction against the remediation investment.

NBA · 2025-26 season · Interactive report

Should You Crash or Get Back?

Does crashing the offensive glass leave a team exposed in transition?

Across all 30 teams, offensive rebounding rate and transition points allowed were essentially unrelated. A team's own turnover rate was the real driver of transition defense, and with turnovers held constant, rebounding had no significant effect. Together, the three factors explained about 80% of the variation in net rating.
