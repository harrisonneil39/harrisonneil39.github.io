Whose Agent Deserves a Raise?

Salary against on-court production for the NBA's highest-paid players, split into four quadrants at the group median.

Method

The Production Index blends VORP, Win Shares and Box Plus/Minus as equal-weighted z-scores, rescaled to 0–100. Players are ranked by salary and by production; the gap between those ranks decides who is most overpaid and who is the best bargain.

Players with fewer than 30 games were excluded, including Anthony Davis, Jayson Tatum, Trae Young, Tyrese Haliburton, Domantas Sabonis, Ja Morant, Kyrie Irving and Dejounte Murray. Data: Basketball-Reference, Hoops Rumors.
