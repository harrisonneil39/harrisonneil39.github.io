How to Use Smart Picks

Everything you need to know to read the output and make the most of the model.

1
Search & Select a Player
Go to the Model tab and start typing any player name, team abbreviation, or position in the search box. The dropdown shows matching results from all 457 real 2024 NFL skill-position players (QB/RB/WR/TE). You must select a player from the dropdown — the Analyze button stays disabled until you do.
2
Choose a Prop Type
The prop dropdown automatically filters to stats relevant to the selected player's position — QBs show passing stats, RBs show rushing and receiving, etc. Pick the prop you want to analyze.
3
Set the Game Context
Enter the opponent abbreviation (KC, DAL, NYG…) and select HOME or AWAY. The matchup history lens (Lens 2) filters the player's 2024 log for games against that specific defense. The home/away lens (Lens 3) filters for the matching venue. Optionally paste in the prop line from your sportsbook for an OVER/UNDER pick with confidence.
4
Read the Four Lenses
01
Last 10 Games
Raw recent form — week, opponent, and the stat. Shows hot streaks, slumps, and usage changes.
02
vs. This Opponent
All 2024 games vs. the same defense. Reveals schematic advantages or matchup traps.
03
Home / Away Split
Games at the matching venue. Some players swing 20%+ between home and road.
04
Defense vs. Position
How much the opponent defense allowed to this position group over their 2024 games.
5
Interpret the Prediction
The model outputs a projected value from an XGBoost regression trained on rolling L3/L5/L10 averages, standard deviation, season average, and defensive context. If you provided a line, you get an OVER / UNDER pick with a confidence percentage derived from bootstrap simulation — 2,000 draws using the player's rolling variability.

60%+ confidence in one direction is meaningful. Under 55% is essentially a coin flip.
